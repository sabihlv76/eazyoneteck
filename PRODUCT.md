# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Eazy1teck is used by the store owner or staff who need to keep a small tech catalog accurate from a laptop or phone. Their main job is to add products quickly, keep prices and images current, and manage the public contact details customers use to book items.

## Product Purpose

The product is a mobile-friendly tech shop with an admin panel for catalog management. Success means the admin can add or update inventory without developer help, and shoppers see trustworthy product details with current pricing, images, and contact information.

## Brand Personality

Direct, practical, and premium. The interface should feel like a working store tool for a local tech business, not a generic AI dashboard or a decorative landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid vague AI-style copy, oversized dashboard heroes, decorative gradients that do not support a task, and admin flows that hide basic product entry behind too much ceremony.

## Design Principles

Put inventory work first.

Keep the product categories visible at all times.

Use plain language that sounds like a real shop operator.

Preserve the storefront's existing category structure unless a workflow needs a more specific admin bucket.

Make mobile product entry feel deliberate, not cramped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, clear labels, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions.
